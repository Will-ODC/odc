Should ODC keep member data anonymous by default?

Most online platforms collect clicks, searches, locations, and interests, then use them to target ads, train models, or sell access to audiences. People rarely control those uses or share in the value created.

What ODC could do differently

What this vote cannot change

Governance ballots always remain secret, unlinkable, and outside commerce. Public positions remain public. This vote applies only to optional research, survey, and sentiment data.

How anonymous data could help members

Members could choose to contribute anonymous data to community-approved research. The findings could improve briefings, show whether projects work, support better public services, or guide new initiatives. Any outside license would need a separate vote, clear consent, and revenue returned to the community treasury.
