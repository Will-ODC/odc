Desktop

Desktop, improved (proposal)

Why

  • The choices move up into the page, right under the question. You read the question and answer it in one column, top to bottom, instead of looking from the top left to the bottom right corner.
  • The bottom-right drawer is replaced on desktop (700px and wider) by the same voting card, placed in the page with no shadow. The phone keeps its bottom sheet, unchanged.
  • The question prints once. The card drops its "Decision" label and the repeated question; the heading above names the choices for screen readers.
  • After you submit, the card folds into the "You picked X." line, and that line carries the one "Change my answer" button. Pressing it opens the choices in the same spot with your saved answer selected.
  • The page is one 680px column centered in the space beside the Answered rail, so nothing sits in a far corner at 1280 or 1440 wide.
  • What changes place: the choices, Submit and "Your answer is private." (drawer to under the question); "Change my answer" (floating button to the end of the "You picked" line); Copy link (under the heading to the end of the closing-date line); Next (inside the results card to just under it, so it stays when results are hidden).
  • What stays put: the results card and its Hide/Show results button, the "Sign in to confirm your vote." line, the closing date, and Home. No wording changes, except a visible "Close" button while changing an answer.
  • The Answered list stays a rail at 1100px and wider. From 700 to 1099px it stays hidden, and My votes carries it, as today.
  • Focus: after Submit, focus moves to the "You picked X." line (tabindex -1, so it can take focus without being a tab stop). "Change my answer" opens the choices with focus on the saved choice. "Close" (nothing sent) puts focus back on the "Change my answer" button.

Phone

What changes in the app

  • The dark page and its see-through panels become white, with 1px gray borders. Only the floating voting popout keeps a light shadow, because it sits over the page.
  • The teal accent becomes one green. Pale green marks "chosen", "yours" and "open". Red appears only for errors.
  • Corners get smaller: 12px for cards, 8px for buttons and fields.
  • The focus ring becomes dark gray, 2px wide with a 2px gap, so it shows clearly on white and on green.
  • The results chart keeps its tested blue and orange.
  • Phones (screens under 700px wide) get the phone version. It uses 16px text, buttons and rows at least 44px tall, Home / About / My votes as tabs under the top bar, and the voting window as a sheet along the bottom edge.

#499 · proposal for review

Dark theme, with a theme button in the top bar

Decisions

  • Same design, darker room. Every colour token gets a dark value; nothing else changes. Layout, sizes, words and the one-green-family rule stay. In-page cards share the near-black page and use 1px lines; floating sheets and popups use one subtly lighter raised surface.
  • Green gets lighter, and text on it gets dark. A deep green on near-black is too dim to read, so the accent steps up to a light green, and the words on a solid green button turn near-black (8.1:1), as dark themes commonly do.
  • Floating things separate from the page. The voting sheet, account panel and popups use --surface-raised (#1a1f1c), keep their 1px line, and use the darker shadow.
  • Chart colours use the dark steps of the same tested blue and orange (dataviz palette, slots 1 and 2), re-validated on the dark surface: all checks pass.
  • The button: one 32px icon button (44px on a phone), no border, last in the bar's right-hand group, after Sign in or Account. A moon in the light theme, a sun in the dark one: it shows where a press takes you. Its name says what it does: "Switch to dark theme" / "Switch to light theme" (also its tooltip). It is an ordinary button, reached with Tab, pressed with Enter or Space; focus stays on it after the press.
  • Why last in the bar: it is the one control in the bar that never changes, so pinning it to the right edge keeps it in exactly the same spot on every page, signed in or out, and on the sign-in page where there is no Sign in link (see the red guide line below).
  • Two-state theme decision (operator, 2026-11-08). First visit follows the device (light or dark in the phone's or computer's settings). A press is remembered in this browser and wins over the device. A press that lands on the device's own theme clears the saved choice, so the page goes back to following the device. If the browser will not store the choice, the button still works for this visit.
  • No flash of the wrong theme: the theme is decided before the page is drawn (a few lines in the page head), so a dark reader never sees a white flash.

#500 · proposal for review

The posted notice, compact

Decisions

  • It moves into the question's column. Today it is a band across the whole main area, above the page. It becomes the first thing in the question's own column (680px on desktop, the screen less 16px each side on a phone), left-aligned with the question, and only as wide as its words: about 330px.
  • One line, three parts: a small check-in-a-circle icon (desktop only; left out on a phone to keep one line) and "Your question is live" in semibold, then "Copy link" as a quiet green button (no box, so the line stays light), then a small × Close button. The pale green ground and green edge stay, so it still reads as good news.
  • "Copied" replaces "Copy link" in place, with a check, for 3 seconds, then the button reads "Copy link" again. The button keeps its width, so nothing shifts. There is no second line. A hidden status message says "Copied" to screen readers.
  • No clipboard: the link appears in a read-only field inside the notice, selected, on a second row within the notice (still inside the column); the hidden status says "Copy the selected link."
  • Close stays at the end of the line: 30px on desktop, 44px on a phone, named "Close" (today "Dismiss": "Close" is plainer). After closing, focus goes to the question heading right below, where the notice was, as today.
  • While the notice shows, the question's own Copy link stays hidden (as today: one Copy link is enough). The notice still goes away when you move on to another question.
  • The question's own Copy link (on any question page) gets the same in-place "Copied", so both work the same way.
