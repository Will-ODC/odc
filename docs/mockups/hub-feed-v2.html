Should we adopt the 0.2% transit levy?

What you're deciding

A 0.2% levy on property tax would raise about $1.4M a year, ring-fenced for bus frequency on routes 3, 7 and 12. It would run for six years, then need a new vote.

Briefing · 4 min

Council modelled three funding options. The levy was the only one that did not reduce service elsewhere. Two independent reviews are attached, including the one that disputes the ridership forecast.
