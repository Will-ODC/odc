What you're deciding

A 0.2% levy on property tax raises about $1.4M a year, ring-fenced for bus frequency on routes 3, 7 and 12. It runs six years, then needs a new vote.

Briefing · 4 min

Council modelled three funding options. The levy was the only one that did not cut service elsewhere. Two independent reviews are attached, including the one disputing the ridership forecast.
