What the states assume, and what they'd cost to change:

  • Two presses to vote, one sheet to confirm. Matches the store as built: first vote stands, no take-backs. If you decide people may change their vote until close, state 3's sheet loses its warning and state 5 gains a "change it" button.
  • Pick one, not many. Every state assumes single choice. Approval voting would turn the circles into squares and the footnote into "pick as many as you like".
  • Nothing here says how a vote is recorded or counted — that rule held through all six.
